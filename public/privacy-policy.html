Privacy Policy

Obsura is designed with privacy-first principles. We aim to minimize data collection and process sensitive content responsibly.

What We Process

  • Text or image content you submit for redaction workflows.
  • Basic operational telemetry required for service reliability.
  • No sale of personal data.

How We Use Data

  • To perform requested redaction and return transformed output.
  • To maintain security, uptime, and abuse prevention controls.
  • To troubleshoot defects when explicitly reported by users.

Retention

Unless explicitly configured otherwise for enterprise workflows, redaction inputs and outputs are handled as transient processing data.

Contact

For privacy concerns, contact security@obsura.one.

Obsura provides private redaction workflows for text and images.
